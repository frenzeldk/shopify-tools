Bin Count Sheet

List the Shipmondo bins to count. Every SKU stored in those bins is looked up in Shopify and printed as a packing slip with its expected on-hand quantity and a blank column to write the counted figure in.

One pattern per line, or separated by commas/spaces. * matches anything (A1-*), ? one character (A1-0?), [1-12] a number range (A1-[01-12]) and [A-D] a letter range.
